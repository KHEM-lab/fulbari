About Our School

Established with a vision to provide quality education in the Banke district, Fulbari Educational Academy stands as a beacon of academic excellence and moral growth. "Fulbari" means garden, symbolizing our commitment to nurturing each student's unique potential and helping them blossom into responsible citizens.

Our dedicated faculty members focus on both academic rigor and extracurricular engagement, ensuring that every student is prepared for the challenges of the modern world.

Our Programs

We offer comprehensive educational programs tailored to different age groups.

PRE-SCHOOL

Play-based learning focusing on motor skills, basic vocabulary, and social interaction in a warm, caring environment.

PRIMARY LEVEL

Building a strong foundation in Mathematics, Science, English, and Nepali, combined with creative arts and sports.

LOWER SECONDARY LEVEL

Focusing on subject-oriented learning and preparing them for upper secondary education.

SECONDARY LEVEL

Advanced curriculum designed to prepare students for higher education and professional success, with modern lab and library facilities.
